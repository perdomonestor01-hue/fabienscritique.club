The Titan Mute Challenge: A Back Seat Chaos Review

The Titan Mute Challenge: A Back Seat Chaos Review

Most people think the front row is the best place to experience a roller coaster, but I'm here to tell you that's not always the case. When it comes to the Titan at Six Flags Over Texas, the back seat is where the real chaos happens.

The Experience

The Titan is a hyper coaster that stands at an impressive 255 feet (about 78 meters) tall, with a drop that generates roughly 4.2 Gs at the bottom. The ride features a unique design, with a steep drop, airtime hills, and a helix that will leave you breathless. As I rode the Titan in the back seat, I felt the full force of the coaster's power. The airtime hills were intense, with about 1.5 seconds of weightlessness that left my stomach in my throat.

What Nobody Tells You

One of the things that nobody tells you about the Titan is that the back row rarely has more than a 10-minute additional wait, and it's worth every second. Most people want the front row for the view, but they're trading the best forces for an unobstructed sightline. The second-to-last row is also almost as good as the last row, with virtually no extra wait. That's the sweet spot if you're doing multiple rides.

My Take After Riding the Titan Multiple Times

I've ridden the Titan in every seat at every time of day, and I can confidently say that the back seat in the last car, first ride of the day, is the definitive Titan experience. The first drop genuinely feels like a 5-G ejector seat. The front row is still a great coaster, but you're experiencing maybe 70% of what the Titan can deliver.
