Why the Back Row on Texas Titan is a Must-Ride

Why the Back Row on Texas Titan is a Must-Ride

The thrill of the unknown is what draws us to roller coasters, and the Texas Titan at Six Flags Over Texas is no exception. With its intense forces and hilarious rider reactions, this coaster is a must-ride for any enthusiast. But what makes the back row so special?

The Experience

Riding in the back row of Texas Titan is a completely different experience from sitting in the front. The forces are more intense, the airtime is more pronounced, and the overall sensation is more exhilarating. I've had the chance to ride this coaster multiple times, and I can confidently say that the back row is the way to go.

What Nobody Tells You

One thing that nobody tells you about Texas Titan is that the queue line can get quite long, especially on weekends. However, the wait for the back row is rarely more than 10-15 minutes longer than the wait for the front row. And trust me, it's worth it. The views from the back row may not be as unobstructed as those from the front, but the ride experience is far superior.

My Take After Riding

I've ridden Texas Titan multiple times, and each time I've sat in a different row. But the back row is still my favorite. There's something about the way the forces build up as you crest the top of the first hill, and then the sudden drop into the abyss that just can't be beat. If you're a fan of roller coasters, you owe it to yourself to ride Texas Titan from the back row at least once.
