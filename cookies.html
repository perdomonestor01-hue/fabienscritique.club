Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 13, 2026

TL;DR: We use minimal cookies - mainly to remember if you've subscribed so we don't pester you with popups. No advertising cookies. No cross-site tracking. Just the basics.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember your preferences and understand how you use them.

2. Our Cookie Philosophy

We believe in minimal tracking. Unlike most websites that use dozens of cookies for advertising and tracking, we only use what's necessary for the site to work properly.

We DO NOT use:

  • Advertising cookies
  • Cross-site tracking cookies
  • Third-party marketing cookies
  • Social media tracking pixels

3. Cookies We Use

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
emailSubscribed Functional Remembers if you've subscribed to our newsletter (prevents popup) Permanent (localStorage)

4. Local Storage vs. Cookies

We primarily use localStorage rather than traditional cookies. LocalStorage:

  • Stays on your device only
  • Is never sent to our servers
  • Can be cleared through your browser settings
  • Doesn't track you across different websites

5. Third-Party Services

Our site connects to some third-party services that may set their own cookies:

Google Apps Script

Used for our email subscription and contact form. Google may set cookies when you submit a form. See Google's Privacy Policy.

GitHub Pages

Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect basic server logs. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

6. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
  • Edge: Settings > Privacy, Search, and Services > Cookies

Clearing Our Data

To clear data specific to our site:

  1. Open your browser's Developer Tools (usually F12)
  2. Go to Application (Chrome) or Storage (Firefox)
  3. Find "Local Storage" > "fabienscritique.club"
  4. Right-click and select "Clear" or delete individual items

7. Do Not Track

We respect "Do Not Track" browser signals. Since we don't track you for advertising purposes anyway, this setting doesn't change your experience on our site.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy if we add new features requiring cookies. Any significant changes will be announced.
