Time & Attendance

CWS Digital Clock

clock.securecwsapplicationsllc.com/hub · timekeeping & punch capture

A digital time-and-attendance hub replaces paper time cards and honor-system punching. For a staffing firm running many hourly workers across client sites, this is the single largest and most defensible source of recoverable cost — it attacks losses that are already happening on every payroll and every client invoice, and it is available 24/7 so no punch, hour, or check goes missing off-hours.

1
Eliminates time theft & buddy punching. ~75% of firms lose money this way[1], averaging 2.2% of gross payroll[2]. A verified digital punch closes most of that leak.
2
Recovers billable hours & kills invoice rejections. Missing punches and missing hours become under-billed or disputed invoices — pure margin leakage. Clean, timestamped records make timesheets invoice-ready and defensible on the first submission.
3
Cuts payroll calculation error & missing checks. Manual time cards carry a 1–8% error rate[3]; automation reduces payroll errors by ~58%[4], so hours and checks reconcile the first time.
4
Shrinks payroll-processing time by ~30%[4] — hours of collecting, reconciling and re-keying time cards every period.

Workforce Portal / Self-Service

CWS Employee App

securecwsapplicationsllc.com · applications, onboarding & self-service

The employee-facing portal — where applications, onboarding, documents and day-to-day self-service live. For a staffing business with high hiring volume, its value is concentrated in administrative throughput: work that HR/recruiting staff would otherwise do by hand, per worker, at scale.

1
Deflects HR admin work. Self-service for pay stubs, schedules, PTO and document access removes a standing weekly load from staff.
2
Digitizes onboarding. Cost per hire averages ~$4,700 with onboarding ~$1,830[6]; digital packets cut admin hours and materials on every one of your hires.
3
Removes paper — printing, packets and postage per new worker.

Safety & Incident Reporting

CWS Safety Manager

safety-report.securecwsapplicationsllc.com · incident capture & compliance

Digital safety and incident reporting. The value here is asymmetric: the admin savings are modest, but the injury-avoidance upside is large because the underlying events are expensive. Faster hazard reporting and cleaner OSHA recordkeeping are exactly the levers that move claim frequency.

1
Speeds incident reporting & OSHA logs — 24/7 digital capture replaces paper 300-log admin and investigation paperwork per event, so nothing is reported late.
2
OHSAS / OSHA recordkeeping accuracy. Structured, timestamped, audit-ready records replace error-prone paper forms — the difference between a clean audit and a citation.
3
Reduces injury frequency. Each medically-consulted injury averages $43,000[5]; safety programs return $4–$6 per $1 invested[7]. Even a modeled 10% reduction is decisive.
4
Lowers compliance & claim exposure — U.S. employers spend >$1B/week on serious injuries[8]; better records reduce that tail risk.

Assessment notes. This is a value model, not an audit of the CWS applications, which were not directly accessible during preparation; product descriptions are at the category level implied by each name and URL, not verified feature inventories. All monetary outputs are modeled estimates derived from the user-supplied inputs and the cited third-party benchmarks; they are not a guarantee of results. Benchmarks are drawn from published U.S. industry sources and represent averages across many organizations — a specific firm's outcomes vary with baseline maturity, workforce mix, and adoption. The largest single line (time-theft recovery) scales with total payroll and is deliberately gated by an adjustable capture% defaulted to a conservative 60%. Injury-reduction value is defaulted to a conservative 10% and should be tuned to CWS's own loss history.
